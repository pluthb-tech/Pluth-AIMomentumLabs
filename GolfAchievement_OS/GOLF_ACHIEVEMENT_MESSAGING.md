# Golf Achievement — Messaging and Offer Brief

Status: proposed strategy; public program facts are recorded in SOURCE_REGISTER.md. Brad must validate this before publishing.

## Brand architecture

Golf Achievement is the business umbrella. Brad supplies the personal expertise. Use BradPluthGolf.com for coach credibility and local coaching decisions; use GolfAchievement.com for online education and enrollment. This is a recommended division of roles, not an audit of the current platform.

## Primary audience hypothesis

Alex is a recreational golfer who wants a dependable plan rather than another unconnected practice session. Alex wants to see what is improving, understand what to do next, and feel comfortable playing with others. Validate with actual inquiries; do not quote this as customer research. Avoid narrowing by income, age, gender, or location without evidence. Local visitors and remote learners need separate routing.

## Offer shape

Retain the existing flagship name. Proposed journey: establish a baseline → choose priorities → practice with purpose → apply decisions on the course → review and adjust. These are operating phases, not a claimed syllabus or allocation of the advertised sessions.

Proposed deliverables: scorecard, practice plan, routine card, decision checklist, progress review. Confirm which are included at each purchase level; do not imply one-to-one coaching comes with self-paced access.

## Draft pitch

Golf Achievement helps you make a plan for the game you have today. Start by finding where shots are going, choose a small set of practice priorities, and bring a repeatable process to your next round. Brad's approach connects the way you move, prepare, practice, and make decisions. Choose local coaching or online learning based on how you want support. We will explain exactly what is included before you commit.

## Message rules

- Lead with a practical next action, not technology or a credential list.
- Show a tracking method alongside every practice recommendation.
- Use encouragement without making golfers feel inadequate.
- Keep junior/family and competitive pathways distinct from the flagship audience.
- Publish only the actual inclusions and current investment; omit inferred savings, urgency, and guarantees.

## Objections to validate

“Will this fit my practice time?” → Discuss a realistic schedule before recommending a program.
“I have taken lessons before.” → Ask what transferred to the course and what did not.
“Can I do this remotely?” → Explain only the confirmed tools, support, and deliverables.
These are draft prompts, not sourced customer quotations.

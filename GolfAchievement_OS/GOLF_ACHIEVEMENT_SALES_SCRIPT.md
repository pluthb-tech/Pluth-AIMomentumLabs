# Golf Achievement — Inbound Conversation Draft

Purpose: help a golfer choose an appropriate path. Status: untested script; no calls audited.

1. **Open:** “What would you most like to change about your golf?”
2. **Baseline:** “What happens in a typical round, and what have you already tried?” Ask for scores only if relevant and willingly shared.
3. **Priorities:** “Where do you feel you lose the most shots? What are you able to practice between rounds?”
4. **Reflect:** Summarize the golfer's goal and constraints in their own terms. Ask if the summary is accurate.
5. **Recommend:** Explain the appropriate confirmed local or online option, its scope, included support, and actual investment. Explain uncertainty honestly.
6. **Resolve:** “What would you need to know to decide whether this fits?” Address the actual question rather than arguing against an assumed objection.
7. **Next step:** Agree on a booking, enrollment, follow-up date, or no action. Record the decision and preferred contact method.

## Responses to likely questions — hypotheses

“Will I break 100?” → “Let's understand your starting point and the work involved. I can explain our actual program and policy, but I won't invent a result for you.”
“I don't have much time.” → “What practice time is realistic? Let's see which option fits before you commit.”
“What is included?” → Use the verified tier comparison; don't bundle personal coaching into self-paced access.
“I need to think.” → “What would help you make the decision? If useful, we can agree on a follow-up date.”

Record: goal, baseline volunteered, format preference, fit, actual offer discussed, questions, decision, and next action. Keep no-fit and not-now distinct.

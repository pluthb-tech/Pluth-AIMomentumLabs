# Golf Achievement — Draft SOP Library

Status: proposed processes, not a record of current operations. Proposed owner: Brad until delegation is confirmed. Review monthly and after an offer or platform change.

## GA-SOP-01 — Inquiry routing

Trigger: new inquiry. Record source and response preference. Ask for the golfer's goal and local/online preference. Check suitability against the confirmed offer. Provide the correct validated next step and record an owner/action date. Do not promise a result or unconfirmed service.
Definition of done: inquiry recorded, route selected, response prepared or sent by an authorized person, next action assigned. Exception: unclear fit goes to Brad; no automatic rejection based on inferred demographics.

## GA-SOP-02 — Assessment preparation

Trigger: confirmed local appointment. Confirm venue, date/time in America/Chicago, session scope, current fee, and applicable cancellation policy. Ask for golf goals and relevant previous practice notes. Confirm any necessary equipment instructions with Brad. Follow the venue's approved process for physical limitations; assistants do not diagnose.
Definition of done: golfer has accurate appointment details and Brad has the goal/baseline notes. Exception: unresolved venue or policy details must be clarified before sending a confirmation.

## GA-SOP-03 — Practice-plan delivery

Trigger: assessment or coaching session completed. Brad chooses priorities. Record the task, frequency agreed with the golfer, measurement, and review date. Separate coach-approved technical guidance from administrative summaries. Prepare the recap using the golfer's preferred permitted channel.
Definition of done: plan approved by Brad, correct recipient confirmed, delivery recorded, next review date set. Exception: an assistant cannot invent a drill to complete missing coaching notes.

## GA-SOP-04 — Progress review

Trigger: agreed review date or golfer request. Compare like-for-like baseline notes and practice observations. Ask what transferred to the course. Brad chooses whether to continue, adapt, or reassess. Ask for feedback; request testimonial permission separately if appropriate.
Definition of done: golfer has a next priority and measurement; review recorded. Exception: missing data is recorded as unknown, not a zero or assumed improvement.

## Safe delegation boundaries

Administrative drafting, routing, and recordkeeping are candidates for support after capacity review. Coaching decisions, policy exceptions, guarantees, and factual proof approval stay with Brad. Automated sending requires a validated workflow, consent rules, and explicit authorization. No delegation, hiring, or automation has occurred in this build.
